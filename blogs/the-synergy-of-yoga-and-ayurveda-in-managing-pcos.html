Your Step-by-Step Daily Healing Yoga Guide

To help you begin your journey toward total hormonal recovery, here are four specific clinical yoga practices. This routine is designed to lower your stress, improve sugar processing, and open up circulation in your pelvis.

1. Supported Reclined Bound Angle Pose (Supta Baddha Konasana)

Duration: Hold for 10 to 15 minutes daily, preferably in the evening.

How to Do It: Lie flat on your back on a soft mat or your bed. Bring the soles of your feet together and let your knees gently fall open toward the floor like the wings of a butterfly. Place comfortable thick pillows under your outer thighs so your inner groin can relax completely without any stretching pain. Place your hands on your lower stomach, relax your shoulders, close your eyes, and breathe naturally into your palms.

Why It Heals: This pose completely removes physical tension from your pelvic floor. It releases deep-seated congestion around your ovaries, lowers adrenal stress, and directs a warm wave of healing circulation into your reproductive center.

2. Left-Nostril Moon Breathing (Chandra Bhedana Pranayama)

Duration: Practice for 5 to 8 minutes every morning before breakfast.

How to Do It: Sit comfortably with a straight spine. Close your eyes and relax your face muscles. Lift your right hand up. Use your right thumb to gently close your right nostril. Inhale slowly and deeply through your open left nostril for 4 seconds. Then, close your left nostril with your ring finger, lift your thumb, and exhale smoothly through your right nostril for 6 seconds. Continue this cycle: always breathe in left, always breathe out right.

Why It Heals: This specialized breathing practice instantly calms down an overactive, stressed nervous system. It lowers circulating cortisol levels, cools down internal systemic inflammation, and regulates the brain-to-ovary signaling pathway.

3. Post-Meal Sitting Pose (Vajrasana)

Duration: Sit for 5 to 7 minutes right after breakfast, lunch, and dinner.

How to Do It: Kneel down on a comfortable floor or your bed. Keep your big toes touching behind you and spread your heels out wide. Sit your hips down into the space between your heels. Keep your upper body tall, rest your palms on your thighs, and look straight ahead. Focus on relaxing your belly with every breath.

Why It Heals: Vajrasana modifies blood flow patterns in your body. It reduces circulation to your legs and routes it directly to your digestive organs, boosting your digestive fire, preventing heavy bloating, and helping your cells manage sugar from your meals efficiently.

4. Supported Legs-Up-The-Wall Pose (Viparita Karani)

Duration: Hold for 10 minutes every evening when you feel fatigued.

How to Do It: Place your yoga mat or a blanket right up against an empty wall. Sit sideways next to the wall, then gently roll onto your back while sweeping your legs straight up against the wall. Your body should form a comfortable L-shape. Slide a soft cushion under your lower hips for support. Let your arms open wide to the sides, close your eyes, and completely relax your leg muscles.

Why It Heals: This pose works against gravity to drain stagnant blood and fluids from your lower limbs back to your heart and pelvic center. It reduces abdominal swelling, brings fresh nutrients to your reproductive system, and induces deep, systemic relaxation.

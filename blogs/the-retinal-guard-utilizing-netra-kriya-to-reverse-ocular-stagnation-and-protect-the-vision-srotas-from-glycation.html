5 'Netra Kriya' Steps for Retinal Resilience

At onlineyogaclass.in, we recommend this sequence performed in the early morning to achieve Biological Scaling of your vision:

1. Horizontal Scanning (Vama-Dakshina)

Slowly move the eyes from far left to far right without moving the head. This rhythmic contraction improves Vascular Hemodynamics in the medial and lateral rectus muscles, clearing Vascular Stagnation in the ophthalmic veins.

2. Vertical Tracking (Urdhva-Adho)

Move the gaze from the ceiling to the floor. This mechanical "shunting" increases Vascular Perfusion to the superior and inferior retinal sectors, facilitating Neural Recovery of the photoreceptors.

3. Circular Rotation (Chakrasana)

Rotate the eyes in full circles, clockwise and anti-clockwise. This clears metabolic Ama from the entire ocular orbit and stabilizes the Thyroid-Ovarian Axis by reducing ocular-derived stress signals.

4. Near-Far Shifting (Drishti-Sanchalan)

Shift focus from your thumb (near) to the horizon (far). This recalibrates the ciliary muscle's accommodation and provides a direct Neural Recovery signal to the HPA-axis, restoring your Lunar Rhythm of focus.

5. Restorative Palming (Anjali)

Rub your palms until warm and cup them over closed eyes. This thermal signal induces deep parasympathetic activation, preserving your Shukra Dhatu vitality and allowing the retinal capillaries to rest and repair.

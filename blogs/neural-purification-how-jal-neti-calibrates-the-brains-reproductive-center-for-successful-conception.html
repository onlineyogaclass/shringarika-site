The "Cooling" Effect: Stabilizing the Hypothalamus

From a neuro-anatomical perspective, the olfactory bulbs are situated just above the nasal cavity and have a direct neural pathway to the Limbic System, which governs emotions and stress. When the nasal passages are congested or the body is in a state of chronic sympathetic arousal, the "thermal" environment of the brain shifts.

The implication of Jal Neti is twofold: it physically removes these pollutants and induces a "Cranial Cooling" effect. This cooling signals the hypothalamus to reduce the production of stress-related hormones like cortisol, thereby prioritizing the release of Gonadotropin-Releasing Hormone (GnRH), the trigger for your entire fertility cycle.

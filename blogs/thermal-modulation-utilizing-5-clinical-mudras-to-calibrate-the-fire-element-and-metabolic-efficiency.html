5 Clinical Mudras for Fire Balance

At onlineyogaclass.in, we use these gestures to achieve Biological Scaling for metabolic health:

1. Surya Mudra (To Increase Fire)

Place the tip of the ring finger at the base of the thumb and press down with the thumb. This increases Metabolic Agni, making it essential for managing Insulin Resistance and weight stagnation in PCOS. It helps "burn" the Ama clogging the channels.

2. Prithvi Mudra (To Balance Fire)

Join the tips of the thumb and ring finger. This stabilizes the fire element by grounding it with the earth element. It is vital for Neural Recovery after a period of intense stress or inflammation.

3. Linga Mudra (To Intense Fire)

Interlock the fingers and keep the left thumb upright, encircled by the right thumb and index finger. This generates significant internal heat, useful for clearing chronic Kapha stagnation and respiratory congestion.

4. Vaayu Mudra (To Cool Excess Fire)

Place the index finger at the base of the thumb and press with the thumb. While primarily for air, it calms the "fanning" of fire that leads to acidity and hyper-thyroid anxiety, restoring the Lunar Rhythm.

5. Apana Mudra (The Fire of Elimination)

Join the tips of the middle and ring fingers to the thumb. This directs the "fire" downward to the pelvic region, assisting in Apana Vayu regulation and efficient detoxification—key for endometrial receptivity.

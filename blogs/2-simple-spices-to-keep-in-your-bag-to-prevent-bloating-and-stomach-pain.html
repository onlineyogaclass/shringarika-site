The 2 Emergency Spices to Keep in Your Bag

Keep a small, airtight vial or spice box containing these two natural gut-soothers in your pocket or bag for instant post-meal comfort:

1. Fennel Seeds (Saunf): The Natural Gas Expeller

How to use it: Take a small teaspoon of raw fennel seeds immediately after finishing a heavy meal. Chew them thoroughly for 1 to 2 minutes until they turn into a fine paste before swallowing.

Why it works: Fennel seeds are rich in anethole, a powerful volatile oil that relaxes muscle spasms in the gut. Chewing fennel speeds up your stomach's emptying rate, prevents gas bubbles from joining together into painful pockets, and refreshes your breath naturally, stopping post-lunch bloating right away.

2. Carom Seeds (Ajwain): The Instant Cramp Reliever

How to use it: When dealing with sudden, sharp stomach ache or heavy acid bloating, chew half a teaspoon of ajwain seeds with a small pinch of black salt or follow with a few sips of warm water.

Why it works: Ajwain contains exceptionally high levels of thymol, an aromatic organic compound that stimulates your stomach lining to secrete an immediate wave of high-quality digestive juices. This sudden surge hot-wires your internal digestion, breaks down heavy restaurant fats rapidly, and releases trapped flatulence to drop painful stomach pressure within minutes.

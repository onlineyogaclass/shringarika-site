Targeted Daily Clinical Postures for Structural and Internal Balance

Our evidence-based programs at onlineyogaclass.in rely on a specific sequence of slow, purposeful holds to open up your deep internal channels and deliver warm, oxygenated blood flow directly to your lower pelvic organs.

1. Supported Reclined Butterfly Pose (Supta Baddha Konasana)

Time to Hold: 10 to 12 minutes every evening right before bed.

How to practice: Rest your spine and back completely flat on a soft mat or bed. Bring the bottom pads of your feet together to touch, and let your knees move wide apart to your left and right. Place thick bed pillows or cushions directly beneath your outer thighs so your groin does not experience any heavy pulling or straining. Place your palms gently over your lower abdomen, close your eyes, and allow your lower body to release completely.

2. Gentle Seated Twist (Bharadvajasana Variation)

Time to Practice: Hold for 3 minutes on each side every morning on an empty stomach.

How to practice: Sit upright comfortably on a cushion with your legs extended straight out. Fold both legs back gently toward your left side, letting your ankles rest near your hip. Turn your entire upper chest and torso smoothly toward your right side, placing your right hand flat behind you on the floor and your left hand under your right knee. Keep your collarbones wide and look back over your shoulder while breathing softly into your belly.

How it helps: Slow, structured twisting movements create a highly efficient compression and release response inside your abdominal cavity. This action stimulates blood flow to your pancreas and liver, encouraging your cells to use sugar energy effectively and clearing away physical stagnation from your lower digestive tract.

3. Supported Bridge Pose (Setu Bandhasana with Block)

Time to Hold: Maintain a relaxed hold for 5 to 7 minutes every evening.

How to practice: Lie flat on your back with your knees bent and feet resting hip-width apart flat on the floor. Lift your hips up gently and slide a firm yoga block or a thick folded blanket directly under your tailbone and lower back for solid support. Let your hips rest their full weight flat down onto the support. Extend your arms out to your sides with your palms open toward the ceiling, keeping your neck long and completely relaxed.

How it helps: Elevating your pelvis creates a gentle, passive inversion that safely redirects blood circulation away from your lower limbs and guides it toward your master thyroid and pituitary pathways. This simple change balances your core internal controllers and restores baseline physical vitality naturally.

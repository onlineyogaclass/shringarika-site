The Complete, Detailed Guide to Your 5-Minute Post-Meal Vajrasana Protocol

1. Form Your Kneeling Foundation Safely

Step-by-Step Instructions: Within 10 minutes of finishing your lunch or dinner, move to a soft surface, a thick rug, or right onto your bed. Gently come down onto both of your knees. Bring your two big toes together behind you so they touch softly or slightly cross over edge-to-edge. Separate your heels out wide to the left and right sides, creating a comfortable, bowl-like space to sit down into.

2. Lower Your Hips and Lengthen Your Spine

Step-by-Step Instructions: Slowly and mindfully lower your buttocks down until you are sitting completely inside the cradle of your separate heels. Keep your back straight, your chest open and relaxed, and rest your palms flat on your thighs, right above your knees. Lift gently through the crown of your head to stack your spine straight over your hips, keeping your shoulders dropped and soft.

3. Practice Slow Abdominal Breathing (5 Minutes)

Step-by-Step Instructions: Close your eyes softly. Take a slow, quiet, deep breath in through your nose, letting your belly push softly forward and expand fully. As you exhale completely through your nose, draw your belly button gently back toward your spine. Keep your jaw relaxed and maintain this steady, deep breathing pattern for 5 full minutes without distraction.

Anatomical Prompt for Seniors or Beginners: If you experience minor stiffness or localized discomfort in your ankles or knee joints when kneeling, place a soft, rolled-up towel right underneath your ankle joints, or slip a flat pillow between your thighs and calves to safely reduce the physical pressure.

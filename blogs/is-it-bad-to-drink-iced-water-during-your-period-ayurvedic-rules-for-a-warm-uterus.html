The 3 Ayurvedic Rules for a Warm, Comfortable Uterus

To ease painful uterine contractions and protect your internal circulation during your cycle, follow these three practical rules:

1. Transition and Stick to Lukewarm or Spiced Infusions

How to do it: Completely pause iced water and refrigerated foods during your cycle. Drink water that is either comfortably warm or room temperature. For optimal relief, sip warm water infused with half a teaspoon of crushed fennel seeds (Saunf) or ginger slices.

Why it works: Warm water acts as a natural muscle relaxant for your gut and pelvic floor, while warming carminative spices widen blood lines, preventing clots and soothing cramps instantly.

2. Rest in Supported Sofa Butterfly (Supta Baddha Konasana)

How to do it: Sit comfortably on your couch or bed with a pillow behind your back. Bring the soles of your feet together and let your knees softly drop open to the sides. Place a warm water bottle or a heated pad over your lower abdomen and rest for 10 minutes while breathing slowly.

Why it works: This fully supported shape uses gravity to relax your inner thigh and groin structures, easing pelvic floor tension and allowing the external heat to dissolve deep muscle contractions safely.

3. Practice 5 Minutes of Alternate Nostril Breathing (Nadi Shodhana)

How to do it: Sit up straight with a relaxed spine. Gently block your right nostril with your thumb, inhale slowly through your left nostril for 4 seconds, block the left, and exhale smoothly through the right for 4 seconds. Then, inhale through the right and exhale through the left. Repeat for 5 minutes.

Why it works: This balanced breathing pattern stabilizes your autonomic nervous system, shifts your body out of sympathetic survival loops, and helps distribute internal body heat evenly across your extremities.
